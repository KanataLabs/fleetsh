Selecting targets
=================
ls, exec, update and reboot accept SELECTOR:

  hk1           one inventory alias
  hk1,sg1       a union of aliases
  '@web'        every host in group web
  '@web,@asia'   the union of two groups
  '@web,sg1'     a group plus another alias
  '@all' or all  every host

Comma-separated selectors are unions, not intersections. Hosts are deduplicated
and sorted by alias. Avoid spaces around commas. Quote @ selectors in PowerShell.
--tag NAME narrows the selected union to hosts carrying that exact tag.

  fleetsh ls '@all'
  fleetsh ls '@web,@asia' --tag production --json
  fleetsh exec 'hk1,sg1' "uptime" --serial

With no selector, ls lists all hosts, including an empty inventory.
Remote actions require a selector. Unknown aliases/groups and selections with
no matching hosts are errors. SSH and local host-edit/show commands take one
exact alias, rather than a selector.

Console-only hosts are inventory assets. They are listed normally and reported
as skipped by batch remote actions; they are never contacted for SSH execution.
Each host can belong to multiple groups; see docs groups to change memberships.
